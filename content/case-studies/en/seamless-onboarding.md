## The Challenge

Every new client engagement triggered a flurry of internal tasks: drafting and sending welcome emails, booking kickoff calls, creating project folders, setting up task management boards, and issuing access credentials. It fell on account managers to coordinate all of it, usually while juggling active projects. New clients often waited days before feeling properly welcomed.

## The Solution

CatalytIQ wired the contract-signing platform directly into the firm's operations stack. The moment a contract is signed, the automation kicks off: welcome emails go out, kickoff calls are scheduled based on calendar availability, project tasks are created in the project management tool, folders are set up, and the new client is added to the CRM with all relevant details populated. Everything is ready before the account manager even opens their inbox.

## Results

- Clients receive welcome communications within minutes of signing
- Kickoff calls automatically scheduled — no back-and-forth emails
- Account managers save 2–3 hours of setup work per new client
- Consistent onboarding experience regardless of who handles the account
