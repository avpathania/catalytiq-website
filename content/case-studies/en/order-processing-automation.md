## The Situation

- Orders arrived across three separate channels — Shopify, a custom website, and direct email — with no unified pipeline. Processing meant downloading each batch, manually entering data into the inventory system, matching to fulfillment, and chasing tracking updates by hand. Two people spent 2-3 hours each, every day.
- With that volume of manual touchpoints, errors were inevitable: wrong items picked, duplicate orders, missed customer communications. The error rate sat at 3-5%.
- Those errors had a real cost — returns processing, replacement shipments, and customer complaints were quietly eating into margins and damaging trust with every order cycle.

## The Solution

- We built an agentic workflow that monitors all three order channels continuously, auto-ingests new orders, validates them against inventory, and sends fulfillment instructions to the partner — no human in the loop for standard orders.
- Shopify, the custom site API, the inventory system, the fulfillment partner, and the customer email system are all connected. Tracking updates fire automatically at key milestones: order confirmed, dispatched, out for delivery.
- The team only sees exceptions — out-of-stock items or unusual orders that need a human call. A daily dashboard shows orders processed, exceptions flagged, and fulfillment status at a glance.

## The Results

- **Processing time per order**: 5 minutes → 30 seconds (10x faster)
- **Error rate**: 3-5% → below 0.5% — rework and returns near-eliminated
- **Cost saving**: ~$15,000–$20,000/month — two FTE worth of order admin freed for higher-value work
- **Margin improvement**: ~1-2% recovered from fewer error-driven returns
- **Scalability**: The system now handles 2x the previous order volume without additional headcount
