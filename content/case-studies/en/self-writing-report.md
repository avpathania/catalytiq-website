## The Challenge

Every Friday, the production manager spent the better part of the morning pulling data from multiple systems — machines, shift logs, quality records, and inventory — to compile the weekly KPI report. It was time-consuming, error-prone, and always a scramble. Key decisions were delayed because the data was never ready until late in the day.

## The Solution

CatalytIQ connected all the relevant data sources — production machines, ERP, shift management, and quality systems — into a single automated reporting pipeline. Every Friday morning, the system automatically pulls the latest data, compiles the KPI dashboard, formats it into a clean report, and emails it to the production manager and leadership team before noon.

## Results

- Weekly report delivered automatically every Friday before noon
- Zero hours of manual data gathering per week
- Consistent report format improves readability and comparison across periods
- Management decisions now made on Friday afternoon rather than the following Monday
