## The Challenge

The finance team was spending hours each week manually processing supplier invoices — opening emails, extracting line items, cross-referencing purchase orders, updating the accounting system, and scheduling payments. Errors crept in regularly, and month-end reconciliation became a recurring bottleneck.

## The Solution

CatalytIQ built an invoice automation pipeline that monitors the finance inbox, extracts structured data from incoming invoices using AI document parsing, matches line items against open purchase orders, updates the accounting records, and queues the payment for approval — all within seconds of the invoice arriving.

## Results

- Invoice processing time reduced from 15–20 minutes per invoice to under 60 seconds
- Data entry errors eliminated entirely
- Finance team freed to focus on analysis and exceptions rather than data entry
- Month-end reconciliation completed faster with clean, consistent records
