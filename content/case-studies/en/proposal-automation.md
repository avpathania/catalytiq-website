## The Situation

- Proposals were taking 2-3 days each — 40-60 hours every month consumed by a manual, people-heavy process: compiling past work, reformatting content, customising the design, chasing reviews, and finally sending.
- The delay was costing real deals. By the time a proposal landed, prospects had already chosen a faster competitor. The sales team was losing on speed, not on quality.
- Senior staff — including the VP of Operations — were being pulled into admin work instead of strategy and delivery, squeezing both capacity and morale across the business.

## The Solution

- We built an AI-powered proposal generator connected directly to HubSpot. When a new deal is created, the system pulls client data, auto-suggests relevant past work and case studies, and drafts customised sections — tone, scope, and service mix matched to the brief.
- Five proposal templates cover the agency's core service packages. The AI selects and adapts the right one, so no one starts from a blank page.
- Quality assurance dropped from a 3-hour group effort (three people rebuilding everything from scratch) to a 30-minute review by one person. Branded PDF, ready to send, in under two hours.

## The Results

- **Turnaround time**: 2-3 days → 2-4 hours (15x faster)
- **Time freed**: 40-50 hours/month — the equivalent of adding a full person to the team
- **Labor redirected**: ~$8,000–$12,000/month previously spent on proposal admin now goes to billable client work
- **Win rate**: Up 12% — faster responses converted more opportunities before competitors could respond
- **Team impact**: The proposal process went from the team's least-favourite task to a non-event; senior staff returned to strategy work
