## The Situation

- The sales team was receiving 100-150 inbound leads a month, but only around 30% were worth pursuing. Every lead still had to be manually researched — company size, tech stack, use case fit — at 15-20 minutes each. Up to 50 hours a month spent on leads that mostly led nowhere.
- Qualified leads weren't moving fast enough either. Response times ran 24-48 hours, and in a competitive SaaS market that delay was costly — an estimated 10-15% of opportunities were going cold before a rep could engage.
- The obvious fix was to hire two or three sales development reps. Cost: $200,000+ per year, plus months of ramp time. The VP of Sales needed a better answer.

## The Solution

- We built an AI lead qualification agent that analyses every inbound lead against a scoring model — company size, detected tech stack, use case signals from form data and public sources. Leads are automatically sorted: demo-ready, needs research, or pass.
- Demo-ready leads receive a personalised first email within the same business day, triggered automatically. A Calendly integration handles calendar matching so prospects can book immediately, without a rep manually coordinating.
- The sales team's queue only ever contains pre-qualified, meeting-ready leads. Research and triage happen before anyone picks up the phone.

## The Results

- **Qualification rate**: 30% → 65% (2.2x improvement)
- **Time to first outreach**: 24-48 hours → under 4 hours
- **Sales cycle**: 35 days → 24 days — faster qualification means deals close sooner
- **Rep capacity**: Each rep handles 10+ more qualified meetings per month without working longer hours
- **Hiring avoided**: $200,000+ in SDR costs made unnecessary
- **Pipeline velocity**: ~$40,000/month in additional revenue opportunity unlocked
