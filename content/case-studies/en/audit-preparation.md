## The Challenge

Every quarter, the finance team spent weeks preparing for the audit: gathering transaction records from multiple systems, reconciling discrepancies, chasing department heads for supporting documentation, and formatting everything into audit-ready packages. The process was exhausting, error-prone, and consumed time that should have been spent on analysis and oversight.

## The Solution

CatalytIQ built an automated audit preparation pipeline that runs continuously throughout the quarter. The system aggregates financial data from all relevant sources, reconciles records in real time, flags discrepancies as they arise rather than at quarter-end, and assembles a complete, formatted audit package automatically. When review time comes, the CFO receives a ready-to-submit report with all supporting documentation attached.

## Results

- Quarterly audit preparation reduced from weeks to hours
- Discrepancies identified and resolved in real time rather than at quarter-end
- Complete audit package generated automatically with zero manual assembly
- CFO and finance team shift focus from data gathering to strategic oversight
