## The Challenge

The sales team was receiving inbound enquiries through the website, email, and contact forms — many of them overnight or over the weekend. By the time someone picked them up, hours or even days had passed. In a competitive market, that delay often meant the prospect had already moved on or chosen a rival.

## The Solution

CatalytIQ built a lead response workflow that monitors all inbound channels around the clock. The moment a new enquiry arrives, the system classifies the lead, pulls relevant context, sends a personalised acknowledgement email, updates the CRM with full details, and generates a draft proposal or follow-up for the sales rep to review. The whole sequence runs in under three minutes.

## Results

- Average response time reduced from next business day to under 3 minutes
- Prospects receive a personalised, contextual reply immediately
- CRM always up to date — no leads lost or forgotten
- Sales team starts every morning with warm leads already engaged
